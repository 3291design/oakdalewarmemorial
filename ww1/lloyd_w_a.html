William Arthur Lloyd, born 19 November 1893, was the son of farmers Edwin and Maria Lloyd of Tir Sais Farm. Leaving behind his quiet rural life, he enlisted in April 1915. By the autumn, he was posted to the divisional signal company of the Royal Marines Divisional Engineers, attached to 2nd Royal Naval Brigade HQ, Royal Naval Division.

As a Sapper, William would have been tasked with the highly dangerous work of establishing and maintaining vital communication lines under fire. In late 1915, his division was deployed to the harsh conditions of Gallipoli, stationed at the Helles Front at the southern tip of the peninsula.
It was here that Sapper W A Lloyd (Deal/1361/S) was killed in action on 30 November 1915, just days after his 22nd birthday. For his ultimate sacrifice, he was posthumously awarded the 1914-1915 Star, the British War Medal, and the Victory Medal. He now rests in Pink Farm Cemetery, Helles.
